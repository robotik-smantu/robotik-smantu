## `01` — WHO WE ARE

> We are a student robotics organization driven by curiosity, creativity, and the passion to build something meaningful.

**Robotik SMANTU** is a space for students to explore technology, develop engineering skills, and turn ideas into real-world creations.

We learn by building, experiment by curiosity, and grow through collaboration.

## `03` — WHAT WE DO

- ⚙️ **Robot Development** — Designing, building, and testing robotic systems.
- 💻 **Programming** — Developing logic, automation, and intelligent systems.
- ⚡ **Electronics** — Exploring circuits, sensors, actuators, and hardware.
- 🧪 **Experimentation** — Learning through prototyping, testing, and iteration.
- 🏆 **Competitions** — Challenging ourselves through robotics competitions.
- 🤝 **Collaboration** — Growing together through teamwork and shared knowledge.
